Thumbpad

Use your Android phone as a trackpad, keyboard, media and volume remote for a Windows PC — over your home Wi-Fi.

Download for Windows

Windows 10 or 11 · free · installs in seconds

Heads up: the installer isn't code-signed yet, so Windows may show a blue "Windows protected your PC" box. Click More info, then Run anyway to continue.

Get set up

  1. Run ThumbpadSetup.exe. It installs Thumbpad and (if you keep the option) starts it with Windows. A pairing window with a QR code opens automatically.
  2. Install the Thumbpad app on your Android phone and keep it on the same Wi-Fi network as the PC.
  3. In the app, tap Pair a PC and scan the QR code on your PC. That's the only time you'll need to pair.
Already installed? Right-click the Thumbpad icon in the system tray (near the clock) to pair again or quit. · All downloads
